Mulberry Foliar Pathology & Sericulture Care Guide

An organic, natural diagnostic reference for Morus alba & Morus indica. Clinical symptoms, fungal life cycles, organic biological remedies, and critical silkworm (Bombyx mori) feed withdrawal protocols.

🔍 Quick Botanical Diagnostic Key (In-Field Flowchart)

Follow natural symptom indicators on the leaf blade to identify foliar conditions

Step 1

Inspect Leaf Underside

Check the abaxial (lower) surface under natural daylight. Look for raised powdery pustules or sunken flat spots.

Step 2 · Rust Path

Brown-Orange Pustules?

Pinpoint yellowish spots erupting into reddish-brown powdery spore mounds indicates Mulberry Leaf Rust.

Step 4 · Pure Green

Lush & Unblemished?

Specimen Profile #01 · Healthy Foliage

Morus alba L. (White Mulberry) — Prime Nutritious Foliage

🌿 Safe for Immediate Feeding

Botanical Morphology:

Healthy leaves display a rich, uniform forest-green hue resulting from optimal chlorophyll-a and chlorophyll-b concentrations. Leaf margins are serrate, venation is reticulate and translucent, and the cuticle possesses a waxy shine that repels waterlogging.

Silkworm Nutritional Metrics:

70–75%
Moisture Content
22–25%
Crude Protein
12–14%
Soluble Sugars

🌱 Agronomic Maintenance Protocol:

  • Split NPK fertilizer (300:120:120 kg/ha/yr for irrigated gardens) applied across 5 rearing crops.
  • Incorporate 20 tons/ha/yr of Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or vermicompost to enrich microbial rhizosphere.
  • Irrigate every 8–10 days with 1.5–2.0 acre-inches of water at root level.
Specimen Profile #02 · Fungal Uredinia

Mulberry Leaf Rust — Pathogen: Cerotelium fici (Cast.) Arth.

⚠️ Withholding Period: 10–14 Days

Clinical Symptom Progression:

Symptoms initiate on mature lower foliage as tiny pinhead-sized chlorotic spots on the upper blade. Concurrently, corresponding spots on the lower leaf surface erupt into bright orange-brown powdery pustules (urediniospores).

Under high relative humidity (80–90%) and warm temperatures (22–28°C), pustules coalesce. The leaf blade turns leathery, loses chlorophyll and moisture, curls inward, and premature defoliation occurs within 10–14 days of sporulation.

🧪 Organic & Chemical Treatment:

  • Sanitation: Manually pluck and incinerate infected bottom leaves before urediniospores become airborne.
  • Organic Spray: Foliar spray of 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Trichoderma viride (0.5%).
  • Chemical Spray: Mancozeb 75% WP (Dithane M-45) @ 0.2% (2g per liter of water) or Copper Oxychloride 0.2%.
  • 🐛 SILKWORM MANDATE: Strict 10–14 days safe waiting period before feeding treated leaves.
Specimen Profile #03 · Necrotic Lesions

Mulberry Leaf Spot — Pathogen: Pseudocercospora mori / Phloeospora maculans

🚨 Withholding Period: 10–12 Days

Clinical Symptom Progression:

Starts as circular to irregular dark brown, black, or purplish spots measuring 2–6 mm across the leaf surface. As necrotic tissue dies, lesions develop a distinct chlorotic yellow halo.

The centers of aged spots turn brittle and fall out, giving the foliage a classic "shot-hole" appearance. Infected leaves exhibit steep drops in protein (down to 12%) and moisture, causing malnutrition if consumed by silkworm larvae.

🧪 Control Measures & Feeding Rules:

  • Canopy Pruning: Thin dense branches to reduce microclimate relative humidity below 75%.
  • Irrigation Etiquette: Eliminate overhead sprinklers; water exclusively via furrow or drip.
  • Systemic Fungicide: Carbendazim 50% WP (Bavistin) @ 0.1% (1g per liter of clean water).
  • 🐛 SILKWORM MANDATE: Observe minimum 10–12 days waiting interval. Wash harvested leaves in clean water prior to tray distribution.
Specimen Profile #04 · Ambiguous & Out-of-Distribution

Unknown Afflictions, Mildew & Viral Complexes

❓ Quarantine Foliage

When MORUS-LENS flags a sample as Unknown / Unidentified Disease, the biological pattern diverges from standard Leaf Rust and Leaf Spot. This safeguard prevents mistaking nutrient deficiencies or viral conditions for fungal infections.

Powdery Mildew (Phyllactinia corylea):

White powdery patches on leaf underside during winter. Spray Sulfex (0.2%) with 5-day safe period.

Mulberry Mosaic Virus (MMV):

Mottling, stunted shoots, and leaf puckering spread by aphids. Uproot infected saplings immediately.

Nitrogen / Iron Chlorosis:

Uniform pale yellow blades with green veins without pustules or necrotic rings. Apply 0.5% FeSO4 foliar spray.

📅 Sericulture Chemical Withholding & Toxicity Calendar

Safety countdown from the day of chemical application until leaf harvest for silkworms

Days 1 – 4
High Chemical Residue

Acute toxic hazard. Feeding will trigger flacherie, vomiting, and 90%+ silkworm mortality.

Days 5 – 9
Residual Breakdown

Active ingredient dissipates through sunlight photo-degradation. Still risky for young (chawki) worms.

Day 10 – 14+
Harvest Approved

Residue drops below biological danger threshold. Safe for 4th and 5th instar silkworm batch rearing.
